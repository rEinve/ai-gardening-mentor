## The quality of help should follow the customer.

Some customers message your business on WhatsApp. Others visit your website and need assistance before choosing a product or service. Both need useful answers about the same business.

Mosi brings those two entry points onto one shared engine. Your approved catalogue, services, prices and policies guide both conversations, so the customer can understand your offer through either channel.

The opening suits the touchpoint: a website visitor may need help exploring; a WhatsApp customer may already have a specific question. The knowledge, sales guidance and rules for involving a person stay consistent.

### Start with what the customer needs

Mosi responds to the actual question and asks for missing details one at a time. It uses what the customer has already shared instead of restarting the conversation.

### Explain the approved offer

Relevant products, services, prices and policies guide the answer. Missing prices, uncertain availability and exceptions go to the responsible person for confirmation.

### Make the next action clear

The customer can compare an option, visit a product page, use an agreed contact route or request follow-up. Mosi asks for contact details when the chosen action needs them.

### Give the team useful context

Where enquiry capture is connected, Mosi passes the customer's need, conversation and unresolved questions into the agreed business workflow. A person can review the enquiry, take over and record what happens next.

## 3. What this means for your business

### Maintain one knowledge base for both customer touchpoints

Use the same approved catalogue, services and commercial information for WhatsApp and website assistance. Keep the offer current at its source and give customers consistent guidance through either channel.

### Collect a useful enquiry

When capture is included in your installation, the team receives the customer's request and the context behind it. A saved enquiry gives someone a starting point for action.

### Keep a person within reach

Mosi acknowledges requests for a person and gathers only what is missing for a useful handoff. When an operator takes over through a connected staff workflow, automated replies pause.

### Answer progress questions from recorded work

With progress tracking connected, Mosi can explain the latest approved update. If the team has not recorded an update, it says so.

### Follow up under your rules

Follow-up can be included where permissions, timing, message rules and stop conditions are agreed. The setup defines who acts when the business owes an answer and when the customer needs a reminder.
