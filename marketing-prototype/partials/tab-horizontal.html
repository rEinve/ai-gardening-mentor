Tomato Lower Leaves Yellowing

1. What It Is: Natural Lower Leaf Senescence

The plant is withdrawing mobile nitrogen from older shaded lower leaves to feed vigorous new upper growth and flower clusters.

2. Immediate Action: 2-Minute Bottom Pruning

Use clean shears to snip off the bottom two yellowed leaves at the stem collar to improve airflow and prevent soil splash.

3. What NOT to Worry About: Zero Blight Danger

This is normal growth biology, not Early Blight. Do not panic-spray harsh chemicals.

White Spots on Zucchini Leaf

1. What It Is: Early Powdery Mildew Check

Fungal spores thrive when leaves stay damp overnight or airflow is crowded around mature squash and zucchini beds.

2. Immediate Action: Morning Root-Zone Watering

Water strictly at the soil base before 09:00 AM, remove heavily dusted older leaves, and keep foliage dry.

3. What NOT to Worry About

Your whole bed is not lost—early physical cleanup and dry foliage stop spread quickly. Need an input? Ask Mentor for nearby agro-dealer contacts.
