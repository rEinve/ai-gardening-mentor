How Do I Get Started With AI Agents for My Business?

Start with one bounded AI support task, prepare knowledge and permissions, test failure paths, and set a clear pilot decision.

BEditorial TeamEditorial teamOct 23, 2026Updated Oct 23, 20262 min read

Choose a suitable first task

Look for a frequent request with a documented answer and a low cost of error. Explaining store hours, checking a permitted order status, or collecting details for a support ticket can work. Avoid beginning with refunds, account security changes, or sensitive advice. Write down the exact point where the task is complete.

Prepare the information and controls

Give the agent current source material and identify who updates it. Connect only the systems needed for the task. Set permissions, identity checks, approval points, and a human handoff path before the first customer interaction. Ask your vendor where conversation data goes, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how it can be deleted.

Test with real variations

Create examples of the normal request, an incomplete request, a changed request, a frustrated customer, a failed tool, and a request for a human. Review both the answer and the action taken in the connected system. Start with staff review or a limited audience, then expand after accuracy and handoffs meet your agreed threshold.

Assign an owner to monitor results and fix source content. An agent is an ongoing service workflow, not a one-time installation.

Set a go or pause rule

Before the pilot begins, agree on the number of cases to review and the outcomes that matter. For example, require correct action records, a reliable transfer to a person, and no exposure of another customer’s data. Decide who can pause the agent when a serious error appears. After the test, review both successful and failed cases with the staff who handle the work. Change one part of the workflow at a time so you can see what improved.

The practical answer

One well-defined task, working handoff, and measured pilot are enough to begin learning safely.
