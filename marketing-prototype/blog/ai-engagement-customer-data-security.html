How Secure Is Customer Data With AI Engagement Platforms?

A practical guide to customer data security in AI engagement platforms: data flow, permissions, prompt injection, vendor evidence, and deletion.

BEditorial TeamEditorial teamOct 23, 2026Updated Oct 23, 20262 min read

Follow the data

Map what enters through chat, email, and voice; where messages and recordings are stored; which model or service receives them; and what appears in logs and analytics. Ask about encryption in transit and at rest, retention periods, deletion, data residency, backups, subcontractors, and whether customer content is used to train models. Review the vendor’s contract and documentation against your obligations.

Limit access and actions

Give the agent only the records and tools required for its task. Enforce access checks in the application and connected systems rather than relying on a prompt that says “do not reveal data.” Require identity verification for private account information and extra approval for sensitive changes. Separate test data from production data, and make staff access auditable.

Test AI-specific failure paths

A malicious message or retrieved document can try to redirect an agent; this is prompt injection. Test whether it can reveal another customer’s information, copy secrets into a reply, or trigger an unauthorized action. OWASP identifies sensitive information disclosure and excessive agency among the risks to review. Monitor access and action logs, have an incident response process, and repeat tests when tools or permissions change.

If a vendor cannot explain the data flow, controls, and deletion path, pause the rollout until those questions have clear answers.

Evidence to request from a vendor

Ask for a current description of security controls, independent assessment or audit reports where available, breach notification terms, and a demonstration of deletion and access export. Check whether customer data can be excluded from model training and whether that setting applies to every service in the chain. Have your security and legal owners review the answers against the data you plan to send. For a first pilot, use synthetic or minimized data until the controls are understood.

The practical answer

Treat the agent as a new route into customer systems: minimize its access, verify every action, and keep data handling visible.
