How Do Customers Feel About Talking to AI Instead of Humans?

Customer preferences for AI and human support vary by task. Learn how to offer choice and measure the full experience.

BEditorial TeamEditorial teamOct 23, 2026Updated Oct 23, 20262 min read

Give customers a clear choice

Say when a conversation is handled by AI. Explain what it can help with, avoid presenting it as a person, and make the route to human support usable. That route should not require customers to repeat the same request several times. A transfer should carry the relevant case history, subject to the customer’s privacy settings.

Read satisfaction in context

Ask whether the issue was resolved, not only whether the reply was fast or friendly. Compare routine requests with sensitive and complex ones. Review both successful AI cases and escalations; a survey sent only after completed AI conversations will miss people who gave up. Repeat contact, abandonment, complaints, and successful transfers can expose friction that a single satisfaction score hides.

Respond to the pattern you find

If customers accept AI for order status but repeatedly ask for a person during cancellations, narrow the automated flow. If the agent gives accurate answers but people still repeat their story after a transfer, fix the handoff. Some customers may need a phone, accessible format, or language support. Treat those needs as part of the service, not exceptions to a preferred channel.

Run a fair preference test

Offer comparable paths to AI and human support for a sample of routine requests, and make the choice clear. Compare resolution and effort by issue type, not only the channel a customer picked first. Include customers who abandon the flow and those who return with the same issue. If a person requests human help, record whether they reached one and whether the handoff preserved context. That tells you more than a broad claim that customers either love or dislike AI.

The practical answer

A useful AI option earns trust by solving suitable requests and making human help easy to reach.
