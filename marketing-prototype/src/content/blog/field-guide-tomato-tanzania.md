## Choosing Bought Seedlings vs. Starting From Seed

In Tanzania, you can start tomatoes either by **buying hardened nursery seedlings** (transplanting directly into prepared beds) or **sowing in a nursery tray** for 25–28 days before transplanting. Both paths work cleanly inside the AI Gardening Mentor Waterfall Planner—your **Expected Harvest Date** and fertilizer prep alerts automatically adjust to whichever method you choose.

## Soil Prep and Basal Nutrition (Day 0)

Before transplanting, work well-decomposed manure into the planting holes and apply **DAP** at the root zone—never touching the tender stem directly. Water thoroughly in the morning before 09:00 AM so seedlings settle without midday heat stress.

## Key Top-Dressing & Foliar Milestones

- **Vegetative Boost (14–21 days after transplant):** Side-dress with **CAN** or **Urea** to support strong stems and canopy leaf area.
- **Flowering & Fruit Set:** Switch to **NPK 17:17:17** and apply a **Calcium-Boron foliar spray** early in the morning to prevent blossom-end rot.

## Calm Triage: Lower Yellowing vs. Early Blight

Do not panic-spray when the bottom two leaves turn yellow on an otherwise vigorous plant—that is natural lower-leaf senescence. Snip those two leaves cleanly at the stem collar to improve airflow and prevent soil splash. Only escalate when dark concentric rings spread upward on young foliage.
