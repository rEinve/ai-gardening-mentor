## The Pain Point: Sudden Crises and Panic Overreactions

When yellow leaves or white spots appear "overnight," beginners often assume total crop failure. They drastically change watering, spray harsh chemicals in peak afternoon heat (scorching the leaves), or abandon the bed altogether.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Camera & WhatsApp Photo Upload:** Inside **Garden Mentor**, tap **`+`** to snap a photo in the bed or upload a photo sent to you via **WhatsApp** from the farm.
- **3-Step Calm Plan:** Every diagnosis is structured to lower stress first:
  1. **What It Is** — plain-language identification (for example, harmless lower-leaf senescence vs. early powdery mildew).
  2. **Immediate Action** — one safe, specific physical or timing step.
  3. **What NOT to Worry About** — explicitly tells you when *not* to panic-spray.
- **Agro-Dealer Lookup (Information Only):** If an input is genuinely needed, ask Mentor for nearby agro-dealers to get verified **Name, Location, and Phone Number**.
