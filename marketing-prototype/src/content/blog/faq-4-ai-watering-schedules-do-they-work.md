## Why Fixed Calendar Reminders Fail

A rigid "water every Tuesday and Friday" alert ignores tropical downpours and heatwaves alike. Watering on top of saturated soil suffocates roots and invites rot.

## How Dynamic Weather Overrides Work in the App

- **Heavy Rain Deferral (`>12 mm`):** When regional weather shows heavy rain, **exposed outdoor garden beds** automatically defer watering tasks so you do not over-water.
- **Sheltered Container Awareness:** Covered balconies and sheltered pots do not catch rain, so those tasks adjust to a quick fingertip soil check instead of skipping blindly.
- **Morning Root-Zone Timing:** Normal sunny-day tasks guide you to soak the root zone before **09:00 AM**, cutting evaporation and keeping leaves dry.
