## When Photo Diagnosis Works—and When It Fails

Photo diagnosis fails when images are blurry, backlit, or too far away—or when an app guesses a scary disease name without checking context.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Handles Leaf Photos

1. **Image Quality Gate (`image-quality-v1`):** Before diagnosing, the app checks whether the leaf is clear enough to inspect and prompts for a closer, well-lit shot if needed.
2. **Camera or WhatsApp Upload:** Snap directly in the garden or upload a photo sent to you via **WhatsApp** using the **`+`** menu in **Garden Mentor**.
3. **3-Step Calm Plan:** Instead of alarmist labels, you get **What It Is**, **Immediate Action**, and **What NOT to Worry About**—distinguishing harmless lower-leaf yellowing from real fungal or pest pressure.
