## The Pain Point: Maintenance Ambiguity and Pruning Errors

Treating a vegetable garden like a once-a-week chore marathon means pests and wilting go unnoticed until damage is irreversible. And when beginners finally prune, they often strip too much healthy tomato foliage—exposing fruit to sunscald—or wait too long to stake vigorous vines.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Rolling 7-Day Care Cadence:** Breaks maintenance into **5-minute daily micro-habits** that work offline and sync when you are back online.
- **Targeted Pruning Instructions:** Triage and cadence cards tell you *exactly* which leaves to remove (for example, snipping only the bottom two yellowed leaves at the stem collar) and which healthy upper canopy leaves to leave alone for fruit protection.
- **Stage-Based Waterfall Milestones:** Know when your crop moves from **Nursery → Planting → Vegetative → Flowering → Fruiting → Harvest** so staking and support happen on time.
