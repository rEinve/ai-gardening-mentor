## Built for Real Tanzanian Garden Beds

The **AI Gardening Mentor Tanzania Pilot** is live for **Android** and **Chrome Web** during our free pilot testing phase. Every personal service—your 7-Day Care Cadence, Waterfall Crop Planner, Garden Mentor chats, and Farm Contact Book—is secured behind your private account login.

## Highlights in This Release

- **Proactive Waterfall Crop Planner:** Exact date math for **Bought Seedlings**, **From Seed**, and **Direct Seeding**, plus **Expected Harvest Date** countdowns and **21-day Succession Planting**.
- **Interactive 14-Day Prepare Ahead Alerts:** Early warnings for DAP, CAN/Urea, NPK 17:17:17, and Calcium-Boron foliar sprays.
- **WhatsApp & Camera Photo Triage:** Upload photos straight from your phone or WhatsApp inside the Garden Mentor `+` menu.
- **Dedicated Farm Contact Book:** Keep **Suppliers** and **Buyers / Clients** organized separately from your personal phonebook.
- **Hybrid Firebase AI Logic:** On-device Gemini Nano support where available, paired with Cloud Gemini fallback and grounded agro-dealer search.
