## The Pain Point: Confusion Around Water, Light, and Soil

Rigid "water every day" schedules cause root rot in heavy rain and drought stress in raised beds during midday heat. Overhead splashing spreads soil-borne fungi onto lower leaves. Meanwhile, navigating **NPK ratios**, **DAP**, **CAN/Urea**, and weak windowsill seedlings creates instant anxiety for first-time growers.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Dynamic Weather Overrides:** On heavy rain days (`>12 mm`), exposed outdoor beds automatically defer watering to prevent root rot, while sheltered containers still prompt a quick fingertip soil check.
- **Morning Root-Zone Habit:** Daily cadence cards remind you to water at the soil base before 09:00 AM—keeping foliage dry.
- **Clear Input Guidance:** Instead of cryptic fertilizer jargon, **Prepare Ahead (Next 14 Days)** names the exact practical input (**DAP**, **CAN/Urea**, **NPK 17:17:17**, **Calcium-Boron foliar**) and when to apply it.
- **Bought Seedlings First-Class Support:** Not ready for indoor seed starting? Choose **Bought Seedlings** in the Crop Planner and skip nursery hurdles completely.
