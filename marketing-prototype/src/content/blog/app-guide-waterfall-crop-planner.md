## 1. Pick Your Starting Method

Open the **Crop Planner** tab and tap **Add Crop Batch**. Choose your crop (Tomato, Cucumber, Bell Pepper, Okra, Ngogwe, Sukuma Wiki, Amaranth, Nightshade, or Squash) and select how you are starting:

- **Bought Seedlings** — skips nursery days and starts right at transplant.
- **From Seed (Nursery → Transplant)** — tracks nursery care first, then transplant.
- **Direct Seeding** — tracks germination and thinning straight in the bed.

## 2. Review Your Prepare Ahead (Next 14 Days)

At the top of the planner, the **Prepare Ahead (Next 14 Days)** strip highlights every upcoming **DAP**, **CAN/Urea**, **NPK 17:17:17**, or **Calcium-Boron** milestone. Tap any alert card to open the action sheet with exact timing and dosage guidance.

## 3. Enable 21-Day Succession Planting

Turn on **Succession Planting** when creating a batch to schedule your next wave 21 days later automatically—giving you continuous harvests across the season.
