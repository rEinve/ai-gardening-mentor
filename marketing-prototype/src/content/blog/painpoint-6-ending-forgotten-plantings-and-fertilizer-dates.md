## The Pain Point: The Record-Keeping and Memory Burden

Almost every grower thinks they will remember when they transplanted tomatoes, which bed received **DAP**, or when **CAN/Urea** is due next—until two weeks pass and the dates blur together. Without records, every season starts from scratch.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Proactive Waterfall Crop Planner:** Logs every batch with its starting method (**Bought Seedlings**, **From Seed**, or **Direct Seeding**) and automatically computes your **Expected Harvest Date** and countdown.
- **Date-Shift Cascade:** If you delay a batch, shifting that single date cascades all downstream milestones automatically.
- **Saved Chat & Search Logs:** Review past diagnoses and agro-dealer lookups anytime via the history viewer inside **Garden Mentor**.
- **Dedicated Farm Contact Book:** Store **Suppliers** and **Buyers / Clients** inside your account, kept cleanly separate from your personal phonebook.
