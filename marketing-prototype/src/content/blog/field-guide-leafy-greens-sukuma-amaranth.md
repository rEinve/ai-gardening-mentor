## Why Plant Leafy Greens in 21-Day Waves

Planting all your **Sukuma Wiki (Kale)**, **Amaranth (Mchicha)**, and **African Nightshade (Mnavu)** on a single day creates one big harvest followed by weeks of empty beds. Staggering batches every **21 days** keeps fresh greens on the table and gives market gardeners steady weekly supply.

## Direct Seeding vs. Nursery Transplants

- **Amaranth (Mchicha):** Direct-seed in shallow drills, thin early, and harvest the first cut around 21–28 days.
- **Sukuma Wiki & Nightshade:** Start in a shaded nursery bed or buy strong seedlings, then transplant at dusk or early morning.

## Shifting a Batch Without Breaking Your Plan

Rain delays or late seed delivery happen. In the Waterfall Crop Planner, shifting a single batch date automatically cascades its downstream **Prepare Ahead** and **Expected Harvest Date** milestones—while keeping your other 21-day waves intact.
