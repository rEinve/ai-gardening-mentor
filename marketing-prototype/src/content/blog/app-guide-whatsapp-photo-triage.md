## Diagnosing Photos Sent Over WhatsApp

You do not have to be standing in the garden to check a leaf. When a caretaker or family member sends a photo on WhatsApp:

1. Open **Garden Mentor** and tap the **`+`** button next to the message box.
2. Choose **Upload Photo from Phone / WhatsApp** (or **Take Photo** if you are on-site).
3. Receive a **3-Step Calm Plan**: **What It Is**, **Immediate Action**, and **What NOT to Worry About**.

## Finding Local Agro-Dealers (Information Only)

If a diagnosis calls for a specific input or seed variety, ask Garden Mentor where to buy it near your region. Using grounded search, Mentor returns local agro-dealer **Name, Location, and Phone Number**—without placing calls or acting on your behalf.

## Saving Contacts in Your Farm Contact Book

Open **Account → Farm Contact Book** to store **Suppliers** and **Buyers / Clients** separately from your personal phonebook.
