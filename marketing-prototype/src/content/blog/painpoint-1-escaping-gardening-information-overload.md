## The Pain Point: Information Overload and Contradictory Advice

New growers rarely fail because they lack information—they fail because they are hit by a **fire hose of conflicting advice**. One video insists you must prune every sucker; another forbids it. Social media "hacks" push shallow egg cartons or gimmicky containers that dry out roots, while generic gardening apps give advice that ignores Tanzania’s climate, regions, and inputs.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Localized 3-Screen Onboarding:** Instead of generic global advice, you select your **Tanzania region** (Dodoma, Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Morogoro, Mwanza, Mbeya, Tanga, Zanzibar, Iringa, or Kilimanjaro), plot type, exposure, and starter crops.
- **One Verified Path:** Every crop rule in the **Waterfall Crop Planner** and **7-Day Care Cadence** is grounded in practical Tanzanian agronomy—no contradictory hacks.
- **Grounded Mentor Chat:** When you ask a question, Garden Mentor already knows your region, beds, and crop stage, so you get one clear next step instead of ten conflicting opinions.
