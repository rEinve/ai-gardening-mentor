## A Pocket Brother, Not a Replacement for Human Experts

No app replaces an experienced extension officer standing in your field or a trusted agro-dealer who knows local seed stock. What AI *can* do is handle the daily 80%—reminding you when to water, when to top-dress, and when not to panic over a normal yellow lower leaf.

## How We Bridge AI and Local Humans

- **Grounded Local Search (Information Only):** Ask **Garden Mentor** for nearby agro-dealers or agronomists in your Tanzanian region and receive verified **Name, Location, and Phone Number**—without the app ever calling or acting on your behalf.
- **Dedicated Farm Contact Book:** Save your trusted **Suppliers** and **Buyers / Clients** inside your account, separate from your personal phonebook.
- **Farm Enterprise Management:** Commercial, greenhouse, and drip-irrigated operations can also work directly with IntelliWebLab for custom onboarding and agronomic support.
