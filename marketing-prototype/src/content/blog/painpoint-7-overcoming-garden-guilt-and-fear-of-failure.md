## The Pain Point: Psychological Toll, Guilt, and Fear of Failure

When a seedling wilts or a harvest falls short, beginners often blame themselves ("I kill everything") instead of seeing normal agricultural trial and error. That guilt is the real reason most first-time gardens are abandoned before harvest.

## How AI Gardening Mentor Solves It

- **Built Like a Helpful Brother, Not a Lecture:** Every screen—from **3-Step Calm Triage** to **5-Minute Daily Habits**—is designed to remove shame and tell you clearly what *not* to worry about.
- **Offline-First Reliability:** Your 7-day routine works even when connectivity drops, so you never lose momentum in the field.
- **Free Tanzania Pilot:** Test the entire workflow on **Android** and **Chrome Web** with zero payment risk—your real-world feedback directly shapes the next release.
