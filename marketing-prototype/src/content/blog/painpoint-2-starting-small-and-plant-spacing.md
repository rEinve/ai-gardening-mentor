## The Pain Point: Overambition and Spatial Misjudgment

Excitement on day one often leads to planting too many beds and varieties at once. Weeks later, weeds and daily watering mount until the garden is abandoned. Worse, beginners underestimate mature plant size—crowding **tomatoes**, **squash**, and **cucumber** seedlings together until they choke out sunlight, trap humidity, and invite fungal disease.
